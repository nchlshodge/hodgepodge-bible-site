The builders in Genesis 11 aren't villains in the cartoon sense. They're ambitious people solving a real problem: how do you keep a scattered population together, safe, and remembered? Their answer was a city and a tower — genuinely impressive engineering for the time.

The line that gives away what's actually going on is quiet: "let us make a name for ourselves." The technology wasn't the problem. The reason underneath it was.

That's worth sitting with every time a new tool shows up promising to connect us, remember us, or make us more than we currently are. The question Genesis is asking isn't "is this technology good or bad" — it's "what are you actually building this to prove, and to whom."
