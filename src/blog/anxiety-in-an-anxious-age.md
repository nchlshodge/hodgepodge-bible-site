Every algorithm on your phone is optimized for one thing: your continued attention. And the cheapest way to get it is to make you a little bit afraid.

That's not a conspiracy theory — it's just how the incentives work. Which means the anxiety you feel scrolling isn't a personal failing. It's a designed outcome.

Scripture doesn't pretend fear isn't real. Jesus tells his disciples "do not let your hearts be troubled" precisely because their hearts *were* troubled — the news of his own arrest and death was about to break over them. The command isn't a denial of the danger. It's an instruction for where to plant your feet while the danger is real.

> "Peace I leave with you; my peace I give you. I do not give to you as the world gives." — John 14:27

The world's peace is the absence of a threat. His is a steadiness that exists *alongside* the threat. That's a much more useful kind of peace for a news cycle that never actually ends.
