Most of what we hear about Noah stops at the flood and the rainbow. We spent this episode on the part in between — roughly a year, cooped up, with no idea when it would end.

Shem, Ham, and Japheth don't get a single recorded word in the whole account. We talked about what it might have been like to be a kid along for a plan you didn't choose, and trusted a father you'd watched build something everyone else thought was crazy.
