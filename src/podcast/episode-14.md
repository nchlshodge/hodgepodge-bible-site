This one started as a simple question from my youngest: "if everything was already perfect, why did Adam have a job?"

We ended up spending most of the episode on Genesis 2:15 — "the Lord God took the man and put him in the garden of Eden to work it and keep it" — and what it means that work shows up *before* sin does. Whatever else the fall breaks, it doesn't invent the idea of work. It just makes it harder.

The boys weren't fully convinced that this means their chores count as "very good." We're calling that an open question.
