Genesis 32 might be the strangest scene in the whole book — Jacob, alone at night, wrestling a figure he eventually realizes is God, refusing to let go until he gets a blessing.

We talked about the limp he walks away with (v.31) as much as the new name. Both stuck. We landed on the idea that wrestling honestly with God, even when you don't win outright, isn't the opposite of faith — it might be what faith looks like at its most serious.
